## Brand & Style

This design system targets deep-tech enterprise partners, venture-backed founders, and technical leaders seeking advanced research, rapid prototyping, and production-grade engineering solutions. The visual tone is authoritative yet kinetic—embodying elite computational rigor, absolute stability, and forward-looking product innovation.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Trust Tech** with **Precision Engineering Aesthetics**:
- **Structured Precision:** Subtle background coordinate grids (24px interval line guides at 3% opacity), crisp technical metric readouts, and sharp informational tags convey disciplined execution.
- **Glass & Luminosity:** Controlled frosted overlays and electric cyan highlights cut through dense deep-navy structural anchors, signaling advanced digital craft without sliding into ungrounded novelty.
- **Controlled Density:** High informational utility balanced with generous negative space ensures complex system architectures, hardware/software telemetry, and multi-tier project portfolios remain immediately readable and frictionless.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between deep foundational navy anchors and high-energy luminescent accents, balanced over a pristine light surface system.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2563EB` - Royal Engineering Blue):** Serves as the primary operational trigger. Used for critical CTAs, active component states, key interactive indicators, and primary links.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Delivers technical resonance. Deployed for real-time status pulses, telemetry highlights, active filter indicators, and high-tech glow accents on glass surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#0B1220` - Deep Void Navy):** The structural core. Drives high-impact contrast panels, technical header bars, terminal shells, code/metric cards, and key brand anchors.
- **Neutral (`#111827` - Carbon Black):** Governs foundational typographic hierarchy, primary icons, and structural divider lines.

### Functional Canvas & Surface Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate Canvas) for overall viewport backgrounds; pure `#FFFFFF` for primary elevated cards and nested modules.
- **Surface Alt / Well:** `#F1F5F9` for data troughs, segmented switch tracks, and code block backgrounds in light contexts.
- **Subtle Border Line:** `#E2E8F0` at 100% opacity for standard light borders; `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)` for active tech boundaries.
- **Muted Text:** `#64748B` for secondary labels, metadata, and structural specs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Space Grotesk** for display titles, section headings, numbers, and technical metadata with **Inter** for sustained reading contexts and deep technical copy.

- **Space Grotesk:** Imparts sharp geometric architecture with subtle idiosyncratic letterforms (visible across numerals, `k`, `g`, and uppercase cuts). Always track display sizes slightly tighter (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project intentional engineering density.
- **Inter:** Chosen for neutral, high-legibility body execution. Its tall x-height and open apertures guarantee strain-free readability across long documentation modules and enterprise specs.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** All labels leverage uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic aerospace telemetry markings and technical hardware stamps.
- **Telemetry & Logs:** For real-time metrics, hex codes, or computational outputs, the system utilizes an optional monospaced fallback (`JetBrains Mono`) locked at fixed line ratios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a strict **12-column responsive fluid grid** anchored by a maximum desktop container width of `1360px`.

### Grid Behavior & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (16px) margins and `gutter-sm` (16px) gutters. Complex two-column layouts reflow cleanly into single vertical stacks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (32px) margins and `gutter` (24px) gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `margin-lg` (64px) canvas buffers and `gutter-lg` (32px) between heavy structural cards.

### Architectural Rhythm
- Sections breathe via alternating rhythm: dense operational cards (using `space-md` gaps) sit within wide-open structural section bandings (using `space-xl` and higher structural wrappers).
- Structural lines can utilize a faint background Cartesian coordinate grid: 1px lines spaced every 48px in `#0B1220` at 3.5% opacity, clipped strictly behind primary interactive containers to avoid visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid approach: **subtle translucent glass layers**, **precise low-contrast technical borders**, and **deep ambient navy shadows** rather than high-contrast physical drops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#F8FAFC` with subtle optional Cartesian line raster overlay.
- **Level 1 (Structural Card):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) surface with a 1px perimeter border in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** `#FFFFFF` surface translated `-2px` on the Y-axis. Perimeter border transitions to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Glassmorphism Nav / Overlays):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Used for sticky headers, telemetry drawers, and floating toolbars.
- **Level 4 (High-Tech Dark Anchor):** Background `#0B1220` with an inner 1px border of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.35)`. Ambient inner glow: radial cyan gradient in upper right corner with 6% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, modern curvature strategy (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Retains operational discipline without sharp, outdated corners.
- **Cards, Technical Containers, & Modals:** `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`). Softens the perimeter while maintaining internal grid alignment.
- **Hero Banners & Flagship Enclosures:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Chips & Status Badges (Exception):** Sharp geometric pill or micro-radius (`0.25rem` / 4px) with structural borders to evoke precision hardware tags and diagnostic components.

- **Deep Tech Card (Dark):** Navy `#0B1220` background, electric cyan hover border (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`), with crisp white headline and muted blue-gray body (`#94A3B8`).
